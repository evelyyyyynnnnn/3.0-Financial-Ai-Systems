Trading Simulation Platform

量化交易模拟与回测平台 — Quantitative trading dashboard with a strategy backtesting engine.

做什么

A second-generation platform, and after the portfolio audit the single trading front end: Express plus Socket.IO for real-time updates, with dedicated services for market data, portfolio state, instrument reference data, backtesting, and — migrated from the retired Investment Analysis Dashboard — investment commentary and the four research pages.

Node.jsExpressSocket.IOChart.jsJestwebpack

Consolidation. Investment Analysis Dashboard 已退役并移入 archive/。其独有的投资建议生成迁入本项目的 /api/advisory/*，四个研究页面迁入 public/research/。迁移时把 Google AI 密钥从源码字面量改为读取 GOOGLE_AI_API_KEY 环境变量。

Repo hygiene. 该项目的 node_modules/ 被提交进了版本库（12,979 个文件）。已加入 .gitignore 防止继续增长，但历史文件仍在跟踪中。
