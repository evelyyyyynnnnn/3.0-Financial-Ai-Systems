Which links actually transmit

Two assets can move together every day and transmit nothing — they just share a factor. What matters for systemic risk is whether a pair fails together. This estimates three things for every pair: ordinary correlation, the lead-lag that orients the link, and tail dependence — how often the target is in its own worst decile given the source is in its. Edge strength is weighted toward the last one.

Method

What this is not. Tail dependence is a statement about co-movement in the tail, not proof of a causal channel. The lead-lag orientation says which side moved first in this window, which is evidence of direction but not of mechanism. Shock propagation is a decay model over the estimated edges — it shows what the graph implies, not a forecast.
