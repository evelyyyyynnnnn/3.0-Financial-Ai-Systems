Coverage is recorded per token so a partial fetch cannot be mistaken for a quiet market. balances are the net of transfers observed in the fetched window. They equal the true register only if the window reaches the token's first block; otherwise an address that has not transacted recently is missing.

What a transfer tape cannot tell you

Price-based liquidity measures are not computed

an ERC-20 Transfer event carries a value and two addresses and no price. Amihud illiquidity and the Roll spread are both built on price changes, so neither can be computed from a transfer tape; and a fund redeeming at net asset value may have no market price to find at all.

The fields are present and null rather than filled with a proxy.
