Volatility Forecasting

深度学习波动率预测 — Deep-learning volatility forecasting with LSTM and Transformer models.

做什么

LSTM and Transformer forecasters for market volatility, with data ingestion, technical-indicator engineering, a training pipeline with early stopping, and Optuna hyperparameter search. All original work — the third-party options project that used to sit beside it now lives under reference/.

PythonPyTorchTransformerLSTMOptunaWeights & BiasesDocker

Third-party code moved out. 此前与本项目并列的 Options-Volatility-Trading/ 并非原创，已移至 project-1/reference/options-volatility-trading/。它是 MIT 许可软件，Copyright (c) 2021 MCF Long Short，来自 Union University 计算金融硕士课程的小组作业。本目录下现在只有原创工作。
