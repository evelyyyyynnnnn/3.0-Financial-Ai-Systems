Financial Network Risk

金融知识图谱与图神经网络风险传导 — Financial knowledge graph analysed with graph neural networks.

做什么

Builds a graph over financial entities and their relationships, then trains GNN architectures over it to surface patterns and propagation paths that pairwise correlation analysis misses.

PythonPyTorchGNNNetworkXpandas
