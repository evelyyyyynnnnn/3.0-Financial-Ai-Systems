Financial Stability

Credit Risk AI

多模态信用风险建模 — Multimodal credit risk modelling across text, market, and image inputs.

做什么

Fuses three modalities into one credit risk model — text (news, filings, social), market data (prices, indicators), and images (charts, visual sentiment) — with configurable architectures and ensemble methods.

PythonPyTorchTransformersOpenCVscikit-learn
