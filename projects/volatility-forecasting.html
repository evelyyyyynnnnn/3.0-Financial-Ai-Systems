Volatility Forecasting

波动率预测与期权波动率交易 — Deep-learning volatility forecasting, plus an options volatility monitor.

做什么

Two separate efforts under one folder. LSTM-Volatility-Prediction is original work: LSTM and Transformer forecasters with Optuna tuning and a full training pipeline. Options-Volatility-Trading is a third-party MIT-licensed project retained as reference — see the attribution note below.

PythonPyTorchTransformerLSTMOptunaWeights & BiasesDocker

Attribution. Options-Volatility-Trading/ is not original work. It is MIT-licensed software, Copyright (c) 2021 MCF Long Short, from mcf-long-short/ibkr-options-volatility-trading — a group project for a Financial Derivatives course at Union University's Masters in Computational Finance. Its src/market_watcher/ib_client/ is Interactive Brokers' official Python TWS API. Keep it clearly labelled as a third-party reference wherever this portfolio is presented.
