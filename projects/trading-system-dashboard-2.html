Trading Simulation Platform

量化交易模拟与回测平台 — Quantitative trading dashboard with a strategy backtesting engine.

做什么

A second-generation platform: Express plus Socket.IO for real-time updates, with dedicated services for market data, portfolio state, instrument reference data, and backtesting.

Node.jsExpressSocket.IOChart.jsJestwebpack

Repo hygiene. 该项目的 node_modules/ 被提交进了版本库（12,979 个文件）。已加入 .gitignore 防止继续增长，但历史文件仍在跟踪中。
