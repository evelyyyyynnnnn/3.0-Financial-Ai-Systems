Three systems · one design constraint

Output you can check

Each of these answers a question where being wrong is expensive — what changed in a company's risk disclosures, which market links actually transmit stress, whether a contract has a known bug class. What they share is the constraint that a person has to be able to verify the answer: every finding quotes its source, every estimate names its method, and every detector states how it produces false positives.

Filing Intelligence

What changed in a company's risk disclosures since the prior filing.

Sentences are matched against the previous filing by containment rather than similarity — so a risk factor kept word-for-word and then quietly extended with a new admission is recognised as the same disclosure, escalated, instead of being misfiled as a new one.

Contagion Observatory

Which cross-market links actually transmit stress, and what a shock does.

Correlation is the wrong instrument: two assets can move together daily and transmit nothing. Edges are weighted toward tail dependence — whether a pair fails together — which surfaces channels ordinary correlation ranks as unremarkable.

Contract Audit

Known vulnerability classes, reported so an auditor can confirm each one.

Every finding carries the triggering line, what an attacker does with it, and the conditions under which this detector is wrong. A tool that hides its failure modes costs more review time than it saves.

How they are built

Pipeline

Python, standard library only. Pulls from SEC EDGAR, Stooq and CoinGecko, or reads Solidity from disk.

data.json

One file per system. Browsers cannot call these sources directly — CORS and per-client rate limits — so precomputation is the architecture, not a shortcut.

Static page

No framework, no build step. The page renders whatever the pipeline last wrote.

Two of the three refresh on a schedule from the repository root: filings monthly, and the 13F tracker in the sibling portfolio. Sample data ships with Filing Intelligence and Contagion so both render before any live pull — fictional issuers in one, simulated series in the other, labelled as such on the page rather than only in a README.
